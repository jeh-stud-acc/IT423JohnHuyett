About me

Why I chose IT

I've been around computers for as long as I can remember. My father works in tech, and growing up I loved tinkering with computers and configuring them to work exactly the way I wanted. That curiosity led me to study IT, and it's still what I enjoy most: figuring out how a system works, setting it up properly, and fixing it when it breaks.

My fall 2026 internship with Marymount's IT department let me do that work for real users. I kept classroom and lab systems running across five buildings, co-led the rebuild of a 24-Mac lab, and wrote a script that fixed a recurring projector problem automatically. That's what draws me to systems administration: finding the fix that keeps working after you walk away.
