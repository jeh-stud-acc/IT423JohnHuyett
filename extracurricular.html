Extracurricular

Leading outside the server room

Some of my best practice at teaching, patience and teamwork happened away from a keyboard.

Volunteer · Youth sports

Coaching a boys' youth basketball team

I coached a boys' youth basketball team. The players came in at very different skill levels, so the job was less about running drills and more about meeting each player where he was while still getting the team to play together.

What I did

Adapted my coaching to each player's skill level, drove teamwork on the court and encouraged each player's individual development.

What it taught me

How to explain the same idea several different ways, and how to lead a group where everyone starts from a different place.

How it carries into IT

It's the same skill I used training new interns and helping users with very different comfort levels with technology.

Community · The Home Depot

Leading Kids Workshop events

The Home Depot runs free Kids Workshops where children build a small project with their families. I led these events at my store.

What I did

Coordinated with store management so every event was fully supplied, ran the workshops, and built relationships with local families and customers.

How it carries into IT

Planning ahead so nothing is missing on the day, and staying patient and clear with people who are learning something new.
